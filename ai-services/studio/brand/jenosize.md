# Jenosize — brand context (draft)

## Voice
- Executive, forward-looking, concrete. Lead with the business implication.
- Southeast Asian context where it is relevant; never forced.
- No hype words ("revolutionary", "game-changing"), no filler openers.
- Never invent statistics, clients, dates or quotes.

## Page design
- One clear headline, a one-paragraph standfirst, then scannable sections.
- Use a pull quote for the single most important idea, at most once.
- Images: a hero image at the top when one is available; inline images only
  where they illustrate the paragraph next to them. Always write alt text.
- Close with a short "What to do next" section of 2-4 concrete actions.
